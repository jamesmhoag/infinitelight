HOW TO PLAY

Enter cellSpace / click your cell to enter nebula stage
Clear nebulaclearing the nebula captures the cell
Escapeleave nebula anytime (pauses state)
Moveclick adjacent cell (ends turn, resets unfinished nebula)
Ringsturn = ring count; new ring each turn
Lockedcells adjacent to captures keep their values
Pass-throughmove through captures in a straight line
△ Merge3 adjacent captures → merged, rings −1
⬡ Merge7 captures (hex) → merged, rings −3
Decayif value < 0.01, oldest capture removed (becomes void)
Descendsurround center void with 3 △-merges
