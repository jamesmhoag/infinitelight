# infinitelight
a discovery based visual art experience

## Play
Served as a static site (GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch `main`, folder `/`).
`index.html` opens the title screen.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | Redirects to the title screen |
| `TitleSplash.html` | Title screen: Continue / Start (New map) / Guide (discovered first), sound |
| `Surface_Map_HUB.html` | The Surface Map: a 3D scene (the hex grid drawn as its ground, a tilting, zooming camera, your ducks swimming round it), day clock, map save, Esc menu |
| `chromatic_nebula.html` | The surface (one level). Runs on its own, or inside the map / title screen with `?hub=1` |
| `join.html` | Play together: a friend's surface, shown as it plays there; your pointer shines and collects on it |
| `content/` | **The game's content, as CSV files you can edit** (and `levels.json`, the levels and prefabs exported from Build mode): tuners (every tuned value and the Tune menu), discoveries, shops and their items and prices, fortunes and wonders, events, Guide entries, ducks, letters, and every other piece of text (`strings.csv`). Every page reads them as it loads; see `content/README.md`. In the text, `{name}` is a value filled in as it shows: `strings.csv`'s last column (`values`) says what each of a row's names is (`name = $0 — what it is`, $0 / $1… being what the code hands it); its `value.<name>` rows at the end are values any text can use (an expression over the tuners) |
| `content.js` | Reads `content/*.csv` (every page loads it first): `csvRead`, and `txt(key, …)` for the text in `strings.csv`; and the loading screen (`loading(why, on)`): black, ∞ with a glowing bead running round it, `load.text` under it with a glow passing over it now and then. It covers the map until it is drawn (the surface's word, every hex's picture, the shops), entering a surface until it is drawn, coming back to the map, and leaving a page, and fades in and out |
| `shared.js` | What the surface and the Surface Map draw the same way — the Dark Remnant's glow, the bow wake, and the rubber duck: its baked meshes and markings (`DUCK_MESH`) and its WebGL renderer (onto a 2D canvas, or straight into a 3D scene) |
| `shops/` | Shop signs (`sign_<shop>`, SVG). The buildings are 3D models (`SHOP_BLD` / `shopModel` in `shared.js`): walls with window and door openings, a 3D shop inside (shelves of goods, a counter along the side away from the door with its front in the shop's colour, drapes in the shop's colour in every window, a lamp), doors that open while the shop's menu is open, glass in every window (drawn as glass: it reflects the map round it, faintly while the lights are on), a neon OPEN sign in a ground floor window while open (`shop.open.sign`), frames, roof, sign scaffolding and the sign mounted on it; lit by the time of day. The shops themselves, their hours and what they sell are `content/shops.csv` and `content/shop_items.csv` |
| `tools/build_duck_meshes.js` | Bakes the two 3D duck meshes (classic, Big Head) and the duck markings texture into `shared.js`, from `tools/Duck_Sheet.svg` (vector views: crisp markings, a lighter mesh, lines and eyes shaded by bump) or, with `DUCK_STRIP=tools/duck_strip.png`, the old pixel strip. First `cd tools && npm install` (SVG rendering), then `node tools/build_duck_meshes.js` after changing the drawing or the pipeline |

**New nebula versions must keep the name `chromatic_nebula.html`** — the map and title screen load it by that name. When you replace it, bump the `&v=` number on its `<iframe>` in `Surface_Map_HUB.html` and `TitleSplash.html` so browsers fetch the new copy instead of a cached one.

## Your buoy, Status & collections
- **Your buoy** (`buoyModel` in `shared.js`, drawn on the map by `buoyDraw`): a striped float half under the surface of the hex you stand on (bright or dark), reflected in it. It bobs, turns slowly, ripples the water, and turns a little faster when the cursor touches it (by how fast it went). Its lamp's spot light circles the hexes next to it clockwise (`mapSpotMs`), and swings onto the hex you point at when you could move there or use the item in hand on it. A move sails it over with a wake; a String fires it there like a Point, with a streak. The sign on top carries your name (asked on a new game: no name yet, or after the Gift Shop's wipe; up to 20 characters, the board as wide as the name needs) and turns to face you; the latest of each souvenir you have kept hangs on its rim. `buoys` holds every buoy on the map, yours first, ready for friends' buoys when the map has players.
- **Status & collections** (click your buoy, or its slot in the map's dock or a surface's inventory): the same menu as the Guide, with its own tabs: **Status**, **Ducks** (one of each type you have, at true size, floating on a pond or adrift in space: a switch toggles them, and Invert turns either white), **Letters** (the book of letters from the bottles) and **Souvenirs**.

## Build mode
- On the title screen, **~** shows **Build mode** (`chromatic_nebula.html?build=1`): a surface for making and testing scenes and prefabs. Nothing in it is saved to your game (items, ducks, discoveries).
- The surface is never cleared there, so Dark Remnants can be placed: one that forms (or is put down with Create) waits where it is, drawn, until **Test**. Remove takes it away again. Rifts don't form.
- Every item is there and endless (∞), everything is revealed, and the item cache shows with only its on / off. Create has a **Surface** type: click to turn the surface dark or bright (white).
- The bar along the top: a level's **name**, **Save** (kept in this browser, IndexedDB `build`), **Load…**, **New surface**, **Blank**, **▶ Test** (the surface plays as it would in a game: its Dark Remnant starts, a Rift can form, it can be cleared; **◼ Back to build** puts it back as it was), **Delete**, **Export** (downloads `levels.json`, every level and prefab: put it in `content/`, where the game reads it as it loads) and **Title**.
- A level keeps everything on the surface (its nebula packed small: one particle in full, the rest only what differs), its Dark Remnants and its ducks, which are collectable when it is played.
- The create tool and the item cache are no longer in a game's ~ panels: they are Build mode's.

## Rifts
- **On a surface** (`riftCheck`, Rift mass in one area): a glass crack runs across the screen at a random angle through that mass, glowing with the Dark Remnant's pulse (`riftCrackMs`), then opens into a jagged pure black stripe (`riftWMin`–`riftWMax` px wide); whatever it opens over is lost. A wave then runs out from it on both sides, parallel to it, and everything is drawn straight across to it and consumed, with the Dark Remnant's bursts where it hits. It is the Dark Remnant's own collapse (`blackHole` with `.rift`: every distance is to its line, `bhVec`), so its settings govern the rest; remnants can still be collected at the last second. Never a duck.
- **On the Surface Map** (`riftCell`): the hex is gone, a scar in the Dark Remnant's pulsing glow, and the hexes beside it are drained (their particles lost, nothing shared out to them or into it). Moves cannot cross a Rift, and a String cannot shoot through one (a straight line only: hexes behind one are not lit as targets). Scars side by side line up into one edge; three that all touch merge into a triangle (`riftsDraw`). They are gone at Midnight (the new map).

## The Guide
Every entry is a card in one format (name, a live demo, page and Tier I–V, then its details and live math, each behind its own discovery). Pages: **Discoveries** (how to play, how to clean up, and the events witnessed), **Taxonomy** (every kind of thing and what it becomes, drawn with the game's own glyphs; its cards below), Items, Shapes, Surface Map, **Shops** (How shops work on top: main street, pop-ups, buying; then each shop's card, only its sign's shape until it has opened, with what it does and a tab for each currency it takes), Ducks, Glossary. (Status, your ducks, letters and souvenirs are in **Status & collections**, below.) A shop's first opening is an event (it is discovered then: the Corner Store and Gift Shop on the first morning, the others when they first open). Discoveries made together come as a hand: one card shown, the others below it to pick; closing it eases time back in slowly (`evResumeMs`).

## Play together
Esc on a surface → **Invite a friend** copies a link (`join.html?room=…`). The friend's page shows the host's surface as a live video (PeerJS / WebRTC, peer to peer; the PeerJS public server only makes the introduction) and sends their pointer. On the host it shines, wipes, mops and collects with the same shine cap (`maxActivePoints`) but its own heat; what the friend collects is added to the friend's own save. Add `&net=bc` to the surface's URL to play between two tabs of one browser (BroadcastChannel, no network).

## Performance
The Surface Map draws its ground on a 2D canvas and sends it to the GPU every frame, so its size is the main cost: `mapPlanePx` caps it, and its sharpness follows the zoom. While the map's window is not the one in use it draws `mapIdleFps` (8) frames a second. A hex's picture is built straight from the surface's particle map (only the cells it shows, its blur passes drawn into the picture, no full frame and no start snapshot): about 12 ms instead of 60, one a frame, so a whole map of new pictures eases in over a second or two. The light moves round to a new time of day over `mapSunMs` (4.5 s). The performance log keeps only the last 600 frames, 25 slowest and 400 events, so a map left open does not grow it.

## Saves (browser storage, per site)
- `chromaticNebulaInventory_v1` — items, resources, discoveries and the duck collection (kept by a new map)
- `nebula-config` — tuners, sound setting, hex-layer tuners
- `il_map_v1` — the Surface Map (pop-up shops live on their hexes)
- `il_letters_v1` — the letters you have found (the Letters book in Status & collections, in the order found)
- `il_name_v1` — your name, on your buoy's sign (cleared by the Gift Shop's wipe)
- `il_pondmode` — whether your duck collection floats on the pond or in space
- `il_souvenirs_v1` — Gift Shop souvenirs (kept through the Gift Shop's wipe)
- IndexedDB `infiniteLight` / `kv` / `session` — the unfinished surface, saved exactly when you leave it

## Keys
- **Esc** — pause menu (sound + volume, readouts, Guide, quit). On the map: sound, Guide, hex-layer tuners, quit.
- **Purchases** on the map show what they did everywhere at once: the feed sums it up (what was spent and what came), each purse and item it changed counts it off and glows (green came, red spent), the time of day lights up when the Clock smith turns it, the row bought flashes, and the Status rows that changed light up
- **Tune** on the map: the Surface Map's own tuners are on their own tab (the map and camera, the day, hex pictures, lake and bottles, shops, pop-up ranks, dock, performance); the Esc menus and the title screen's menu are drawn as a shop's list
- **~** (or H) — hide / show the panels, on a surface and on the Surface Map
- **Surface Map**: Modes (Telemetry: each hex's value, coverage and ring on the ground; Stats: fps, frame times and the hex grid; the Performance log) and Tune (every tuner, as on a surface). Lake ducks (and bottles): brush one slowly and it spins, hit it fast and it putters off, click it and it is dunked; how fast it spins, how hard it is pushed and how much it turns as it goes all follow how fast the cursor moved. Now and then a message in a bottle floats in (`mapBottleMs`): a 3D clear, green or brown glass bottle (drawn by the duck renderer, `bottleModel` in `shared.js`), half under the water and reflected in it. It drifts like a duck; clicking it opens a letter (`LETTERS`), or, for one bottle in four (`mapBottleDuck`), a duck, which joins your collection (one you did not have bobs up onto the lake). A duck's bottle is gold for an exotic, opaque white for a classic, and shines when the duck is new to you. Bottles float upright, leaning, about half under; an empty one can be dunked like a duck. A brush or a hit counts once, until the cursor has come fully off; a dunk sinks it under the waterline and it bobs back up, and the empty bottle goes back on the water. Letters found are kept (`il_letters_v1`) and read in the **Letters** book (Status & collections)
- **~** (or H) — hide / show the panels (everything but the time of day, the inventory and its feed), on a surface and on the Surface Map: one setting for both. They start hidden
- **Rest** — at Midnight you are too tired to move: a Rest button under the time of day ends the day, so a last thing can be done first (a surface, a shop, an item)
- **Surface Map dock**: pointing at an item names it and says what it does; with it in hand, the feed says what it would do on the hex under the cursor and what it costs. Every button (the ▾ too) has its own see-through backing. The **▾** hides the feed and reduces the dock: each button then stays down out of the way until you point at its own place, it is in hand, or it was just used, changed or toggled (`mapDockLingerMs`). ~ leaves the dock and the feed as they are (it hides only the ▾)
- **Surface Map**: Modes (Telemetry: each hex's value, coverage and ring on the ground; Stats: fps, frame times and the hex grid; the Performance log) and Tune (every tuner, as on a surface). Lake ducks (and bottles): brush one slowly and it spins, hit it fast and it putters off, click it and it is dunked; how fast it spins, how hard it is pushed and how much it turns as it goes all follow how fast the cursor moved. Now and then a message in a bottle floats in (`mapBottleMs`): it drifts like a duck, and clicking it opens a letter (`LETTERS` in `shared.js`)
- **Spindle** (armed on the map): a cleared hex back to a surface, or an open hex below 1.00 wound up to 1.00 and held there until cleared. An unwound surface keeps its clear's look: a bright one starts white (its particles back), a Dark Remnant one dark
- **Merges** on the map are one shape: the merged hexes fill edge to edge with only their outer edges drawn
- **Camera**: held on the spill, not the source: it zooms to fit the spill with a margin of lake round it (`mapLakeMargin`) for a bottle or a duck to swim into, follows it as it grows and shrinks, and stops dead at its extent (a rectangle round the hexes' corners plus the margin; no spring back). Zoom in and it stays where you put it until you zoom back out. At a day's end it moves once, smoothly, from where it is to where the next day starts (its middle and zoom), over the collapse
- A bright clear collects everything left on the surface at once
- **Create → Duck** (on a surface): Duck in space floats one of any kind there; Duck on the pond sends it to swim on the Surface Map's lake (it bobs up when you are back on the map)
- **Space** / click your hex — enter its surface
- Map: drag to look around (the source stays in view); right-click to come back to your hex

## Shops (Surface Map)
- **Main street** (turned so the front of each in front of the source, and the back of each behind it, runs along the source hex's edge; round the source: two behind it, two in front; always there, lit only while open): Corner Store (24 hours), Clock smith (Morning – Dusk), XACT change (Dawn – Afternoon), Gift Shop (entering it wipes the save; souvenirs are bought from your last Bright and kept). A closed shop's glass can be tapped (it rings and rattles; five quick taps and someone inside shouts at you); with the Shining Duck's good fortune, Friendly Neighborhood, a tap opens it up off hours.
- **Pop-ups** open on a bright clear whose surface collected enough of one thing (rank 1–3 by the `mapShop<Kind>1..3` tuners: a small store, a tall one, then the wide two-storey one, the biggest). One of each: a better rank moves the shop to the new clear and the old one is torn down. A Dark Remnant clear that earns one opens it on the nearest open bright hex or free spot in the source hex. Every turn, each cleared surface that earned a pop-up and has none open tries again (a hex a shop moved out of, a dark clear that found no room, a holiday). Every shop is drawn at main street's scale (`mapStreetSize`); the two-storey stores of ranks 2 and 3 are drawn larger (`mapShopUpScale`, 1.7) so their doors match main street's. Which opens first: the DUCK SHOP, then the best-ranked exotic one (NEUTRINOS, MAGNETS, the PULSE; a tie at random), then BRiGHT IDEAS, last Dim's.
- **Dim's Carnival of Discovery** (its secrets), each once a day: a Card of Secrets (50 bright: pick one of three face-down cards; what it hides, an event or Guide entry you have not found, is discovered for good and its own card is shown; gone from the menu once nothing is left), Fortune Telling (200: a good fortune named for a duck, or a dim one, for the day), a Daily Wonder (2,000: keep one of three bonuses with a catch). The draw and the card picked are shown by the surface over the map, in the one card format: what it changes in play set apart (monospace, ruled) from what is said of it (the flavour), and a live demo — a good fortune's own duck on the lake, a dim fortune's Dim or Dark Remnants (or the map rule it bends), a wonder's item at work.
- A shop's menu lists everything at once (a long list makes it taller): a tab for each thing it takes, by its symbol (named in the feed), every purse in the dock with the open tab's lit, the item under the cursor described in the feed, and Status at the right.
- **DUCK SHOP** also sells Luck Charms (+5% duck chance today, +1% on the next drop, ×10 the price each one, back to 5,000 each day) and the Lucky Duck (1,000,000, for good: base duck chances ×2, each miss +4%).
- Today's fortune, wonder and charms, and the Keys a fortune took, are the run's state in the surface's save (`run`); the **Status** tab of Status & collections (and the panel beside a shop's menu) shows them with the wallet, its caps and the upgrades bought.
- Duck types (`DUCK_TYPES`, surface): colours (six, then ☆ Crystalize and Eclipse, then ★ Rainbow), sizes (Microscopic, Wee, Tiny, Small, Big, Huge, Massive, Mega, ★ Big Head), materials (Wood, Silhouette (a Dark Remnant's beating glow round its outline, as it turns), Paper, Glass, Metal, Dim, Bright, Shining, ★ Gold). A roll picks rare (`duckRareChance`), then mid (`duckMidChance`), then the other exotics.
- Ducks: every Dark Remnant that is not a duck adds `duckLuckStep` (1%) to Duck chance, every classic duck adds it to Exotic chance; a hit rebases it. The luck is saved with the inventory (`luck`), so it carries across surfaces and days.
- Every item is a discovery (`dc_si_<id>`, Discoveries tab). One-time upgrades are kept as the discovery `up:<id>`; purse-cap upgrades raise `invMaxFor`.
